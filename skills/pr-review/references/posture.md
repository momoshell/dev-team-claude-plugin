## Posture by tier

One reviewer is the standing posture. A write surface touching the **protected floor** boots the lane at the **judge** tier; compute that before dispatch, never after. Judge-tier and protected-floor changes use more than one independent reviewer, and record any disagreement according to `references/divergence.md`.

That panel flow is **parked**: its trigger is the **verdict-fusion** capability
existing, and this file is the panel's knowledge layer, not its implementation.
Do not describe a parked capability as if it had already run.

Size the posture from the measured lanes: the first review bounces **68 of 194**
ledger lanes (F3), and **42 of those 68** pass at round 2. The acceptance gate is
green on **196 of 203** first rounds (F19). A second reviewer buys the most where
the gate is quietest.
